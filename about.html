Our Story

Building Reliable Trade Pipelines Across Three Continents

Founded in 2009 and headquartered in Dubai Airport Free Zone (DAFZA), DB Group FZCO was established with a singular purpose — to make global commodity trade simpler, safer and more reliable for buyers and suppliers alike.

Over 15 years, we have built an extensive network spanning origin markets in South America, South Asia and Southeast Asia, and destination markets across MENA, Sub-Saharan Africa and the wider Asian subcontinent. Our model combines direct supplier relationships, in-house logistics expertise and deep regional market knowledge.

Every shipment is backed by certified quality documentation, third-party inspection, and full regulatory compliance — so our clients never face surprises at port.

Our Journey

15 Years of Growth & Milestones

From a focused commodity desk in Dubai to a globally recognised trading house — here's how DB Group has grown.

2009

Founded in DAFZA

DB Group FZCO is established in Dubai Airport Free Zone with a focused mandate to trade sugar and edible oils across the MENA region.

2013

Expanded to Africa

First major contracts with Sub-Saharan African buyers, extending the trade network to Nigeria, Kenya, Tanzania and Mozambique.

2016

ISO 22000 Certification

Achieved HACCP and ISO 22000 food safety certification, formalising our quality management systems and unlocking new institutional buyer relationships.

2020

1M MT Milestone

Reached the landmark of one million metric tonnes cumulatively delivered — a testament to the scale and reliability of our supply chain.

2024

30+ Country Network

Our active buyer and supplier network now spans 30+ countries across MENA, Africa and Asia, supported by 50+ trade and logistics specialists.
